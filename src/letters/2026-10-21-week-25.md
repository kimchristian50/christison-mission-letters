Why choose one when you can be all three? I have taken on some new roles in the last week! One of our besties wants to get married so she can be temple ready! We're going to be helping her pick out a wedding dress soon! Also, our number is now marked as spam, so we have to tell all of our friends to save our number so we can continue to get in contact with them. Hooray. Here's some more things that happened this week!

Thursday, 10/15-- We had a good church tour lesson with our friend Anthony! We called him p-day night and he said he would love to come to church with us and to grow closer to Jesus Christ! Yay!

Friday, 10/16-- We had a really amazing miracle where we met a surprise member! (I love it when that happens!) We wanted to have comp study at a boba shop (because who wouldn't) and Snow, one of the girls working there, came up to us and said "You guys are Mormon? I'm a Mormon!" (Obviously she meant "member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints") She told us she had been waiting for us to find her ever since she moved to St. Pete a year ago!

Saturday, 10/17-- Our weekly bike day!! It was raining almost all day, which was so fun to bike in!

Sunday, 10/18-- A lot happened on Sunday, so you're going to get some bullet points for it (trying something new!). It was a crazy day, but overall it turned out pretty good (see every day I've told you about as a missionary for some similar examples).
- It started to get a little cold!! We walked outside to go to church and it was 68°! Yay!
- Phil, Aaliyah, Debbie and Snow were able to come to church!
- We went over to the Nichols for some service and Sister Nichols gave us some amazing pumpkin roulade. She made two batches-- one of which didn't crack! See mom, it can be done!
- Our friend Jennifer was going through a lot Sunday night and we ended up calling our DL, STLs and President Hollist as to what we could do. We ended up meeting with her at the church with the Elders, who gave her a priesthood blessing (here's a great sermon on what those are: Miracles, Angels, and Priesthood Power). We talked with her and helped her feel our Father in Heaven's love for her!

Monday, 10/19-- We had a really fun member meal with a couple whose last name is a close second when compared to Christison (the Dolphins 🐬). I got to use a fun object lesson my dad sent me, and Brother Dolphin promptly ordered 50 of them :)

Tuesday, 10/20-- We made a few really cool friends! We stopped by Yvonne, who was a referral that the Sisters helping us with referral reassignment couldn't get in contact with. She opened the door and we had a great impromptu lesson with her, her bestie Amanda, and Amanda's kids! They are so cute and I'm so excited to get to know them better!

Wednesday, 10/21-- We had zone p-day today! All two districts in the zone met up at a park and we ate and played ultimate frisbee.

Spiritual thought-- 1 Nephi 3:7
And it came to pass that I, Nephi, said unto my father: I will go and do the things which the Lord hath commanded, for I know that the Lord giveth no commandments unto the children of men, save he shall prepare a way for them that they may accomplish the thing which he commandeth them.
