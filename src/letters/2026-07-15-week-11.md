We invited one of our friends to the stake volleyball activity that happens every week, but he would much rather play table tennis. The title of my email is the text he sent us when we sent the information for it.

Anyways, I'll try to keep most of the week short because Sunday's going to be long.

Thursday, 07/09
We had dinner with this super cute family in our ward! We had enchiladas with green chile!

Friday, 07/10
We had some missionaries from North Carolina call us and tell us about their recent convert who had just moved to Ocala. Unfortunately, he lives just outside of our area, so we sent the information to the Elders.

Saturday, 07/11
We had a church tour lesson with our friend who likes table tennis, and one of the wards had a full-on concert going on in the gym. At first, it was nice songs like Amazing Grace and Never Enough from the Greatest Showman, but it progressed into loud rock music (live drumset included). It was really hard to hear our friend and I had no idea what we were talking about.

Sunday, 07/12
Now for the much-awaited rundown of what happened on Sunday

So many of our friends came to church, including Lovie and two of her darling kids, ALL of Joel's family (for the first time ever), Arthur and Jeremiah, and Mollie (this cutie lady that the Elders gave us a couple of weeks ago).

As we were pulling away from the church building, the Sisters in the next area over informed us over text that they had an emergency they needed to call us about. They had a baptism in an hour and the 2XL suit that they had didn't fit. They asked us to check if we had a 3 or 4XL, so we turned around to go back to the church. There, we asked the Elders if they could check for one in the men's bathroom. We did have one, so we drove to a rendezvous in their area and gave them the suit.

I got to bike in the rain! It was so fun, but we were wet and dirty when we showed up at our member meal with Bishop and his family 😬

We went home after some more biking, and I helped myself to some homemade otterpops (see recipe at the end of the email).

Monday, 07/13
We had dinner with Louise! She's so funny and the food was delicious!

Tuesday, 07/14
Our friend Javon showed up to institute!! We had to leave partway through to get to a lesson, but he was still there when we went back to the church about an hour later to swap cars with the Elders! That means he had stayed for the rest of the Gospel study and the basketball at the end! Yay!

Wednesday, 07/15
We went to the World Equestrian Center. It's this super posh horse place with a lot of expensive things you can buy if you're filthy rich. It was kind of boring and really hot, but it was cool to look at!

Spiritual thought
Prayers work! We were really worried about this lesson we had with one of our friends, so we asked the district to pray for her and put her name in the temple prayer list. When we had the lesson, it went as good as it could've realistically gone! God really does hear and answer prayers!

Happy p-day,
Sister Christison

Homemade Otterpop Recipe (makes one serving)
Ingredients
1 cup grapes

Directions:
1. Wash grapes
2. Place grapes in ziploc bag
3. Freeze overnight or throughout day

Note: For best result, give your car to some missionaries near you and bike to all your appointments. Otterpops will take on an extra sweet flavor, and will be much more satisfying to eat.
