No daggers or cheese were involved with this park, but I did get to see the ocean for a while, right where the mission boundaries end. Pierce disagrees with President about where the mission boundaries end: President says the mission ends where sand begins, but Pierce thinks there are still valuable contacting opportunities all the way 12 miles out to the international boundary.

Until white shores, and beyond, a far green country under a swift sunrise,
