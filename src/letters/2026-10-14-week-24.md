Remember my friend Caleb when I was back in Ocala?? He is now my first friend that I've taught that has been baptized! He was the one I told you about that was on date for baptism in the past, had something come up, and reached out to me and Sister Crowther as soon as he was able to reach out to missionaries again! He moved out of Ocala soon after I left the area, and he got baptized in Alabama on September 27th! Yes, I know it's been a while-- I just remembered I never told you guys. See my emails for weeks 13 and 15 for more info on him!

Thursday, 10/08-- I got a referral in the best way possible! My MTC teacher served in the Florida Tampa Mission-- the same mission I'm in, for those who don't care to remember something as trivial as where I am right now-- and he served in the St. Pete area-- my current area (man you guys really don't care about me)-- during his time as a missionary! He emailed me about Masi, a friend he had while he was here, and we made plans to stop by and get in contact! More details to come! :)

Saturday, 10/10-- We biked for hours because Google maps thinks we're twice as fast as we actually are. We had to get saved by the Hermanas after our last stop because Sister Robison came down with a really bad migraine. 

Sunday, 10/11-- One of the Seminole Elders' friends accidentally came to our church service instead of the one up in Seminole. We were really confused at church, but Elders called us that night and asked us if a Doyle had showed up at church. Everything made sense after that :)

Monday, 10/12-- We stopped by Masi! He's really nice, and we set up a time to come by again! Stay tuned for what will happen next week!

Tuesday, 10/13-- We put our friend Debbie on date for baptism! She is the cutest and she is so excited to be able to have the blessings of having a covenant relationship with our Father in Heaven!

Spiritual thought-- President Christofferson's talk from General Conference
So many people I've talked to in the last week love this talk! It is so bold and states our viewpoint on gambling so clearly. Even though I don't personally struggle with gambling, I still learned a lot from this talk because I asked myself what I was doing that was taking time away from coming closer to the Lord. I invite you to think of something that you are doing that you could replace with reading the scriptures or spending time with your family (or whatever else you feel prompted to do). I promise that as you do, you will be able to grow closer to our Savior Jesus Christ and become a better person through Him. Let me know any other thoughts you have!
