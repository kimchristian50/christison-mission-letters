We went to the temple three times this week! We did endowments on Tuesday, parent-child sealings on Friday and endowments again this morning. The first two trips were to the Mt. Tempanogos temple and the one today was at the Provo City Center temple.

I'd also like to congratulate my brother and his new wife as of today! Pierce and Jess I love you both so much and I would've loved to be there with you today (in more than just cardboard cut-out)!
