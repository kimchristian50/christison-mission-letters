I'd have two nickles! Which isn't a lot, and it's kind of weird it hasn't happened more times than that. (I've had a lot of experience with people who don't know where New Mexico is)

What's this? An email from your favorite missionary on a day that is not her p-day? What a pleasant surprise!

I got to go to the temple today, so my p-day was moved to today. President just started giving permission to certain people to leave the mission boundaries to visit a temple! There isn't a temple in our mission yet (there's one in Tampa that's under construction, but it won't be finished until late 2027ish). Those in the far south of the mission can go to the Ft. Lauderdale temple, and those in the far north (me!!) can go to the Talahasee temple!

Anyways, I'm sure you're all dying to read about what happened this week, so here you are!

Thursday, 06/04
We had a lesson with our recent convert Leona ❤️ She's so sweet.

Friday, 06/05
We had a lesson with our new friend Sarah! She's literally the sweetest person ever. The first thing she did when we came up to her door was give us some water! Throughout the lesson, she told us about people she knows who would be interested in learning about the Book of Mormon! So she's pretty awesome :)

Later in the day, we wanted to check up on our friend Ruth because we hadn't been able to get in contact with her via phone for a bit. She was sitting on her porch with her neighbor Linda. We prayed with them and had a quick lesson. Linda is the BEST (she's tied up there with everyone else in this email)!! She and Ruth love Jesus Christ and Heavenly Father sooo much!

We had the BEST member meal ever!! This cute couple invited us up to the gated farming community they work at. We sat outside next to some horse pastures and ate enchiladas! They also sent us home with some homemade banana bread 😋

Saturday, 06/06
We had a lesson with our friend Albert, who accepted the invitation to be baptized! We put him on date for July 5, so pray that he'll be able to keep that commitment!

Sunday, 06/07
Albert came to church!! 🥳 🎉

The ward missionary leader's son got baptized! It was super awesome and I'm so glad I was able to see it.

We had a very successful biking day! We talked to a bunch of people and a couple of them were interested in learning more about Christ's Gospel!

Monday, 06/08
Monday was only the BEST DAY EVER!! We had a lot of AMAZING lessons, so I'll put a couple of them here.

We found Ritchie while we were knocking in a trailer park a couple of weeks ago. He had something early the next morning, so we didn't have much time to talk. He basically said "I love Jesus, but I've got to go. Feel free to come back later!"

So next week, we came by. He wasn't home, but his wife Jen was and she gave us his number. We texted and set up a time to come by!

On Monday, we stopped by his house. They had totally forgotten about the appointment and were about to go grocery shopping. But they let us in and we had a great lesson! It was mostly Ritchie bearing his testimony and explaining the spiritual situation he's in right now. They've been living here for a year and they've been going from church to church, trying to find one that feels right. They found one that seemed good, but it wasn't very family-friendly (they've got the three cutest little kids I've ever seen). So last week (when they stopped going to that church), they prayed to God to help them find the right church. Ritchie said they told God that they would go to the next church He pointed them towards. Sooooo they're the best! And I'm so excited to see them at church on Sunday!!

It is so cool to be the answer to someone's prayer in this way! It really strengthens my testimony that God knows and loves individually, and will bring people into your life that you need at the very moment you need them.

Ok, now about Linda and Ruth. They are the CUTEST!! Linda made us dinner ❤️❤️❤️ and we had a great lesson about God's Plan of Happiness. I'd love to say more about what this is, but I've already made this email really long. I'll write another email talking about it and I'll send it to everyone who asks!

Tuesday, 06/09
We got to cross TWO mission boundaries today!! We drove from ours into the Jacksonville mission, then into the Talahasee mission. It was soooo much fun! And the temple was also AMAZING!!! I'm so blessed that we were able to make this trip! Sister Crowther and Sister Tuft (one of the missionaries on ward share with us) haven't been to the temple in 15-16 months. I'm never going to take having a temple nearby for granted again.

Florida is SO GREEN. Every corner you turn it gets a little bit greener-- it's crazy.

Spiritual thought
I've never felt God's love for His children to such a degree as I have during my mission. When we start a lesson with someone-- it doesn't matter who they are, what they look like or how they act-- I feel this immense love for them and a desire to help them come closer to Christ so they can feel it too. There is nothing so rewarding as being able to do God's work and feel that love every day.

I know God loves you so much-- more than you could ever imagine. He wants you to come home and be with Him again.

Love you all!! ❤️❤️❤️
