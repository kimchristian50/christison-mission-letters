Happy National Chocolate Macaroon Day, to all who celebrate!

Things I forgot to mention or explain well last email that are probably important:
- Sister Crowther and I are on wardshare, meaning there is another set of Sisters that teach people in the upper half of the ward boundaries, while we take the bottom half
- I'm driving 🚗 😁 (it is unusual for missionaries to drive during their first transfer, but I get to anyway. Watch out on the roads in Ocala!)
- We are on car share with the Hermanas and two sets of Elders, so we have two biking days a week 🚲 ☀️ 😓
- The biking days aren't actually that bad because they're on Sundays and Wednesdays. Sundays aren't bad because the Sisters we share a ward with bring us to church. Wednesdays are p-days, so the Sisters drive us to all our p-day activities and we bike from 6-9 after p-day ends.

Thursday, 05/28
We had a lesson with our friend Ruth! She's this sweet older lady that we found knocking last week. She had taken lessons with missionaries a while ago, and she loves Jesus Christ so much-- it's so cool to see!

Friday, 05/29
Barbara and Kenny accepted the invitation to be baptized! It was so cool to see them start making this commitment in their lives. Both of them already love Jesus Christ so much and want Him to be more present in their lives!

Friday was also my month mark! I'm officially 1/18th done with my mission, which isn't a lot, but, as my dad says, "it's a fraction that we use in math."

Some words of wisdom from Sister Crowther: "A month of your mission is like a month of your life!"

Saturday, 05/30
At around 1pm, one of the bishopric members asked if I could give a talk in church (which starts at 9am on Sunday), which was only 20 hours in advance. We had a full schedule Saturday afternoon and evening, so I didn't have time to write it that night.

Sunday, 05/31
I started writing my talk at around 7:30am before ward council. I paused writing my talk during ward council, and I finished it while on the stand during the hymn right before I was supposed to speak. It actually came out all right! I talked about how we can become better people through Jesus Christ's Atonement.

None of our friends were able to come to church! 😭

Monday, 05/32
We had a lesson with Barbara and Kenny! They're literally the best and were the highlight of my day ❤️

Tuesday, 05/33
We had a lesson with Monica. It went really well and she was really nice!

Wednesday, 05/34 (man, May is long)
We ate at Panera, then we went to a park with some pickle ball courts and a rope course.

Highlights/Weirdlights:
- Sister Crowther introduced me to a game she and her companions played last summer: Shirts vs Skins. You give each side points out based on if the person who opens the door is wearing a shirt or not, if they're nice, and if they schedule a time to meet with us again.
- We were driving up a bridge that had a walkway and a billboard to the side of it. Sister Crowther was looking at a mini soda can ad on the billboard and said "the mini cans are so cute!" I had not seen the ad, but I did see a Hispanic young man on on the sidewalk. Her comment concerned me because I thought she had said "Dominicans are so cute!"
- I put together my first hurricane bag
- The handlebars on my bike broke, so we had to hitchhike with the Sisters to the Elders so they could fix it
- The rain is awesome!
- The only mission- approved music is what we can find on Gospel Library (the app with all of the church's materials), so that means I get to listen to a LOT of youth music. I've only been here for a week and a half and it's already starting to get on my nerves because it's just about the only music I've heard the entire time.

Spiritual thought:
Alma 7:11
"And he shall go forth, suffering pains and afflictions and temptations of every kind; and this that the word might be fulfilled which saith he will take upon him the pains and the sicknesses of his people."

I love this scripture because it tells us that Christ suffered all of the pains and afflictions that we go through. One of my friends told me once that Jesus knows our pains perfectly, so He knows how to help us perfectly. I know Christ is always there to help us when we reach out to Him.

Love you all, have a good day!
- Sister Christison
