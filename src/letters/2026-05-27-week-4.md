Hey everyone, remember how in my last email I said I would send another on Monday? I hope so, it wasn't that long ago. Well, regardless of whether or not you care enough to remember when you'll next hear from me, that statement was a lie. I apologize for those who waited anxiously for my email on Monday to no avail. Annnd this email is much longer than it would've been if I had sent it two days ago. And YOU get to read it! Lucky you!

As of Wednesday, 5/20 (my first day in the field), p-days are on Wednesdays. So that means my emails are going to be on Wednesdays from now on.

Well, I made it to Flordia!

First, a brief(ish) overview of what happened at the MTC before I left:

Sunday, 05/17 (The Day After P-day)
We had a departing missionary devotional, where all the missionaries leaving that week met together to hear from the MTC presidency. I particularly enjoyed Sister Owen's-- the MTC president's wife-- remarks because she is really funny. At the end of the devotional, we sang We'll Bring the World His Truth, which is a really powerful song about missionary work (if you couldn't tell by the title).
 
We had what's called a "go and do experience," which is where you spend a few hours studying alone-- with your companion within sight and sound, of course. It's a recurring event that happens every Sunday, but I think I forgot to mention it in my last email. I had a nice time listening to music (this is the only time you can listen to music in the MTC), thinking, reading scriptures and journaling.

After that, we went to another devotional with the Owens. I shall spare you the details because I know you'll get lazy and skip the rest of this email if I keep describing my experiences in detail or rambling on and on and on and on and on about things that don't apply to you. Just remember: you signed yourself up for these emails, therefore it's your fault if you don't end up liking them or if they waste your time because they're waaaaaaaaaay too long and take a while to read.

Monday, 05/18 (The Ghost of P-day Future)
We had our last classes as a full district.

Brother Condie, one of our teachers, ate dinner with us at the cafeteria.

We said goodbye to two of our Elders, who left for Arizona early the next morning.

Tuesday, 05/19 (
We had a devotional from Elder Sergio R. Vargas and his wife, Sister Andrea Vargas. They talked about how we should savor our time as missionaries because we are doing the Lord's work, and it is really fun. Then we had a district council without our district leader or his companion (they were the ones that left-- keep up), and during which no one mentioned anything about whether or not they liked horses.

We said goodbye to the rest of our district :(

Wednesday, 05/20 (What Should Have Been P-day)
Sister Nelson and I woke up at 3:50 to catch our 4:30am shuttle to the Salt Lake airport. We got to see Sisters Boren and Hall at the airport! We had a 9:00 flight to Cincinnati, and our flight to Tampa was boarding when we got off the plane. Luckily, the Cincinnati airport is small, so we were able to make it to the gate just in time to board.

Our travels ended at ahout 5:30 Tampa time (which is two hours ahead of NM time).

Now about FLORIDA
(Still Wednesday)
We took pictures with Phoebe the Giant Flamingo at the airport.

We had dinner with the Mission Presidency.

We got shipped to our overnight apartments (at this point I was separated from my dear companion Sister Nelson 😢).

Thursday, 05/21 (Transfer Day, not P-Day-- Theres a Difference)
My first transfers were on Thursday! I got sent to the Ocala zone, which is the northmost zone in the mission. I'm excited because my dad served in the southern part of the Jacksonville, Florida mission, which now overlaps with the northern part of mine. Also, one of the Sisters in my MTC district told me that her aunt lives in Ocala, so hopefully I'll run into her!

I also met my wonderful companion and trainer, Sister Crowther! She's a lot of fun and I'm looking forward to spending this transfer with her!

Also-- I'M DRIVING!!! Sister Crowther is unable to drive, so that means that I get to! It's fairly uncommon for first transfer missionaries to be the designated driver because the training is usually in the third week, so they can start driving as early as their second transfer. But I got an accelerated version of the training and got to drive around a bit when we got to our area. 

There's another set of Sisters in our ward (it's called ward sharing if I ever bring it up again). We live in the same apartment complex, go to the same ward, but cover different parts of the ward boundaries. Some members had all four of us go out to Cracker Barrel with them. 

Friday, 05/22 (I Don't Know how you got This Day Confused With P-day)
This was my first full day of proselyting! First we went to see Tony, who wasn't home. Buuut his roommate Tony (yes, another Tony-- there are three of them in that household) was there, so we told him a bit about the Book of Mormon and asked him if he'd want to meet with us again. He said yes, so hopefully we'll be able to see at least one of them again soon!

We then had a lesson with Deborah, who loves Jesus, but has difficulty getting to our church, so pray for her!

We also had a lesson with Barbara and Kenny, who are AMAZING! They've been to church once before and are committed to keep coming!

We had a lot of appointments that were canceled without our knowledge or any communication from them about why (aka ghosting).

Saturday, 05/23 (The Ghost of P-day Past)
Today was notably not p-day. You would have known this if you had payed attention to my last email.

We did a 5k(ish) with the other missionaries in our district and some members.

We had a lesson with Tony 2, but it fell through while we were driving to his house.

Sunday, 05/24 (The Ghost of P-day Eve)
Sundays are usually P-day Eve, but p-day is no longer on Mondays. Sister Crowther made sure to keep reminding me of this, and I kept reminding her that it had been longer since I last had p-day than it had been for her.

Lovie wasn't able to make it to church due to complications with transportation :(

Sunday was mostly a lot of meetings and knocking. The Elders had the car, so we were biking and walking.

The best part of Sunday was knocking in the rain. Sis Crowther said we might as well just jump in the pool. So when you hear that missionaries can't go swimming-- they can, it just has to be in sky water and not collected ground water.

Alsoooo I cold called someone (well, lots of people), and one of them picked up! We scheduled a time to call her again and tell her a bit about Jesus Christ. Lots of fun!

The Hermanas (the Spanish-speaking Sisters) gave us Mexican candy when we picked up the car :)

Monday, 05/25 (The Ghost of P-day Present)
As you will have noticed from your woefully empty inbox, Monday was not p-day.

Monday's best lesson was with Willie. We were standing on his porch getting eaten alive by ants when we knocked on his door. He came outside, and sat in his camping chair outside his door. We started talking a bit, and asking how his day went-- and all the while my feet were on fire. I could feel the ants going underneath my sandal straps-- overall an uncomfortable experience. But as soon as we said the prayer to open our lesson, all the ants left my feet and did their own thing. We had a great lesson about Jesus's Atonement and how it applies to his life. Since he currently attends a different church, we also talked about the similarities and differences of his church as opposed to ours. He seemed to be learning a lot!

Tuesday, 05/26 (P-Day Eve)
We had a couple lessons and a lot of canceled lessons. We stopped by an older Sister who's in the ward (for a lot longer than we meant to-- she's pretty chatty). She's really nice and a lot of fun to talk to! She's also really sweet and had her husband pick up little cakes at the bakery for us the night before we came.

All our non-member lessons fell through except for Lovie's, but it was a good lesson so all is well. Her sister-in-law was at her house, so she joined the lesson. She was really interested, participated a lot and even asked for a Book of Mormon at the end of the lesson! Unfortunately, we're on backorder for Book of Mormons, so we told her we'd give Lovie another one for her as soon as we get some.

We knocked on a door of someone who wasn't home (we know this because he told us himself).

The day you were all waiting for! You are all very welcome for sending you this amazing email that you read all the way through without skipping or skimming through anything. And heaven forbid any of you read it after the day I sent this email! Shame onto you and your family (unless you're related to me, in which case shame unto you and all your family members who are not currently serving their mission in Tampa, Florida)!

I got Messenget set up, which took waaay too long. Also, I don't have Facebook because the Hollists (mission presidents) took it out when they came to the mission!

My district went to Silver Springs and I got in a tree.
