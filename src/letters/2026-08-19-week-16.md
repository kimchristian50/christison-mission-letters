We get a lot of referrals in this area. Some of them are from people that we already have records for (they sent in a referral in the past or had contact with the missionaries at some point). There's this lovely feature in our area book that allows us to merge these records so every interaction with the same person is in one place. The Elders did not take advantage of this feature. Not just the Elders we replaced-- I don't think any Elder ever in St. Pete merged any records. There's a bunch of duplicate records-- almost all of our friends and referrals have 1-2 past records. I merged our referral Kimberly seven times!! To my missionaries reading this email: merge your dots!!
