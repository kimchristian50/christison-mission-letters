This was an eventful week! We got to go to the temple again today, so that's why I'm emailing on Tuesday again.

Thursday, 07/16
We had Zone Conference! We talked about inspired questions and how to teach on first contact.

Friday, 07/17
We went to girls camp! The Gainesville stake came down to around our area and we got to join in for about an hour! It was at this really pretty campground right next to a lake (which we weren't allowed to go in, but I got some pretty good pictures of it).

Sunday, 07/19
All of Joel's family came to church again! Arthur and Jeremiah were able to make it as well :)

We had exchanges! I stayed in Ocala with Sister Canning. We had a member meal and a great lesson with our friend that some Sisters in Georgia gave us! His name is Robert, but he prefers to go by Shorty (I wonder what his relative height is. Is it, perhaps, short? 🤔).

Tuesday, 07/21
We unexchanged at 6:30am. We woke up at 5:45 to start driving away at 6. As soon as we got back to the apartment, we picked up the other Sisters and headed to Talahasee.

We went to the temple! It is so peaceful there and I'm so glad we were able to go. I drove the 2.5 hours there, then I got to relax in the back seat the rest of the time :)
