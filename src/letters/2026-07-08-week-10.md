Happy 4th of July! I hope everyone had a good week, though I can't say I did. This week was exhausting, and my social battery is critically low. I am still adjusting to not having very much alone time. But here's my life story from the last time you heard from me! Hope you enjoy it :)

Thursday, 07/02
The transfer started out pretty rough on Thursday. Sister Crowther woke up  with a really bad cold-- we think she got it from her mom through the phone because they were talking all day Wednesday. Those darned Bluetooth colds get you when you're least expecting them.

Friday, 07/03
Almost all of our lessons fell through! (the exclamation mark is sarcastic, for those of you with no sense of humor). We were supposed to meet with Jeremiah (an inactive member that we've been meeting with) at the church. At the last second, he told us he couldn't come, but that we could meet at his place and talk at his doorstep like usual. Five minutes into the lesson, he got a call from his cousin. He picked up and talked for 5-10 minutes while we were standing there awkwardly (and trying not to pass out if you're Sister Crowther). We ended the lesson soon after he hung up because Sister Crowther needed to sit down in the car, and I don't blame her-- it was oppressively hot and humid and she was still a little sick.

Saturday, 07/04
We talked with Marion, this sweet older lady. She loves sci-fi and fantasy books, so she and I are going to have some good conversations 😊

Sunday, 07/05
LOVIE CAME TO CHURCH!!!!! Sister Crowther and Sister Baird started teaching her a few weeks before I came out, and we've been trying to get her to church this whole time (so 7-9 weeks). For all of last transfer, we had a ride set up, and everything was good on our end, but she fell through at the last second every time. But we had a church tour lesson on Friday, which really helped! Our Sunday school lesson corroborated so well with what we've been teaching her these last few lessons, and she loved it so much! She's never going to miss a week, now :)

Also, two of Joel's kids came with him to church! Joel is a returning member, and he is starting to recognise how many blessings come from living the Gospel of Jesus Christ. His testimony is growing stronger each week and his kids and wife are starting to see how the Gospel can bless their life!

Monday, 07/06
We made the Elders think they locked their keys in the apartment. They left the apartment keys on the car key fob and maintenance locked the door while they were out. On our way to their complex to save them, we took off the apartment key and left the mail and bike lock keys. They thought they'd locked the keys in the apartment. It was pretty funny.

Tuesday, 07/07
Tuesday was pretty rough. But we did get to go to institute because our recent convert and his sister were there. So that was pretty fun!

We also had a member meal with the cutest couple in the ward. They are both so funny and so fun to be around!

Wednesday, 07/08
We went to a park and played some pickleball. It rained just enough to make it more humid, so we went to the church after that game. We played Secret Hitler with the district and some other Elders in the zone. One of the Elders brought a lizard into the church and almost dropped it 🤦‍♀️
