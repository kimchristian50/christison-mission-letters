My first transfer is officially over (tomorrow)! Every six weeks in the mission field is called a transfer, and that is when missionaries change around. Transfer news: Sister Crowther and I are both staying here! I'm really glad this is the case because I love my area and I love Sister Crowther ❤️

Honorable mentions of the transfer
- Being on back order for Book of Mormons for the first few weeks
- Zone conference!
- People asking Sister Crowther and I if we're blood sisters all the time
- Seeing Sister Nelson at specialized training!
- SISTER HOWARD-- she's this sweet older lady that we visit once a week and she gives us cake every time we come :)
- Hermana Rich and Sister Tuft-- two of the most amazing missionaries I know that went home this week

I'm not going to do a day by day analysis of what happened this week like I usually do because that's a lot of work and I don't have the time or motivation to do that right now. But I'll put in some highlights of the week, so it'll be a shorter email (you're welcome).

My hands have acquired a cool tan from biking-- the bottom and tips of my fingers are tan, and the middle part of my fingers are not. I noticed this when I was washing my hands the other day and realized that I had a stripe of lighter skin across my fingers.

Sister Crowther and I refilled the gas on all four cars this week-- all three in the car share and the one the Sisters gave us for p-day.

We found Barbara and Kenny! We weren't able to get into contact with them for the longest time, but we felt prompted to stop by their house when we were biking on Sunday. They were on their porch, so we prayed with them and set up a time to see them later in the week! They had been with their family for the last few weeks and they had gotten back a few days before we saw them. On their way home, Kenny told Barbara that he missed his church Sisters and hoped that we would get in contact with them when they got home :)

At the end of my time at the MTC, Sister Nelson gave me a sticker that says "God is within her, she will not fall," which I put on my phone case because it's really cute. Over my time in Florida, the protective film peeled off and the "not" rubbed off, so now it says "God is within her, she will fall."

This is Sister Crowther's last transfer! She's really amazing, and I'm so happy I was lucky enough to have her as a first and second transfer trainer.

Spiritual thought:
I love this scripture from the Book of Mormon. One of my favorite names for Jesus Christ is the Rock because it reminds me that, if He is the foundation of my testimony, nothing can shake my faith. I'd like to invite you guys to reread your favorite story or parable about Jesus Christ (the ten virgins is a good one to read if you can't think of one off the top of your head) and think of ways you can strengthen your testimony of Him.

Love you all!
