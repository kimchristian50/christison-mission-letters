Happy Father's Day, all! I love you dad ❤️

Thank you to everyone who has been writing to me! I love hearing from you guys, so if you ever want to tell me anything, I'd love to hear (or read) it! I'll also try to respond! I can't promise that it'll be in a timely manner, but I'll get to it eventually :)

Thursday, 06/18
We had dinner with Sister Marquez-- she's the sweetest lady in our ward. She made us meatloaf and potatoes.

We had a lesson with Lovie, then a zoom call with Albert. We were running short on time, so we had Albert's lesson in the parking lot of Lovie's apartment complex. Her boyfriend came out a couple of times during the lesson, which was awkward because there we were, in his parking lot 30 minutes after our discussion with Lovie had ended 😬 But the lesson with Albert was great! We had Sister Marquez join us, and she shared a really powerful testimony about how families can be together forever.

We were leaving Lovie's apartment parking lot when we saw Oobie! He's a member of the ward that got baptized about two years ago. We pulled off to the side of the road to talk with him. He was waiting for the bus with his cousin Eric, so we introduced the Book of Mormon and talked about how much Jesus and Heavenly Father love us. He really liked it, so we set up a time to meet later in the week!

Friday, 06/19
We went to this apartment complex to stop by someone Sister Crowther and Sister Canning had found during exchanges. We saw her outside her door and asked if she was still interested in hearing a message about Jesus Christ. She wasn't, namely because she's a Satanist. But, we ran into three people at her complex that love Jesus and were interested in hearing more about Him!

We had chicken alfredo with the Crofts :)

Saturday, 06/20
I survived my first bike crash! I'm totally fine and my bike is completely unharmed, but my slacks got a huge hole on the knee 😢 There was a roadkill armadillo getting eaten by a monarch butterfly and I got a tiny bit distracted. Luckily, we weren't too far from the apartment, so we walked home to change my slacks before going out proselyting again.

We were supposed to have a member meal with this cute older lady in our ward, but something came up and she wasn't able to have us over. But she bought us pizza, which was waiting in our fridge (where Sis. Tuft and Sis. Frey put it) when we got home :)

Sunday, 06/21
We had a member meal with the cutest family in the ward! (are you sensing a pattern...?) Their table is too small for all of us, so they set up plates on a blanket on the floor. It was so much fun and the kids were just adorable!

It was Father's Day, so we didn't have any lessons. We spent a decent amount of time knocking this cute complex and doing contacting on a bench by the lake in the courtyard.

Monday, 06/22
You'll never guess what happened-- we had another member meal! Louise made us burritos, and she had jalapeños and hot sauce we could add on 😁 She's is a recent convert (a member of our church for less than a year) and she is literally on fire! She's on such a spiritual high and is studying the Book of Mormon in depth-- more in depth than I've ever studied it-- so she can understand it better and get closer to Christ!

We had Louise participate in our zoom lesson with Albert! Of course, zoom chose that day to act up worse than it has before, but I think it still ended up going pretty well.

Tuesday, 06/23
We had a great lesson with Eric! I know it's been a while, so I'll refresh your memory: he's the guy we talked to when we ran into Oobie. He is able to discern miracles in his life, and it was so cool to hear some of his stories! He also loved hearing about Joseph Smith and the First Vision.

We had our last member meal of the p-day week! They took us out to a cute pizzeria where the World Cup was on! I couldn't see it very well, but my mom told me the results of the game today :)

Wednesday, 06/24
Our district for the day was five Sisters! The Elders went to the temple yesterday, so they did the same thing we did a couple weeks ago. Sister Tuft and Hermana Rich were at the Fort Lauderdale temple with the other departing missionaries, so Sister Frey, Sister Smith and Hermana Anderson are in a trio right now. So Sister Crowther and I went shopping with the trio today! It was pretty fun 😁

Spiritual thought
It's been so cool to be invited to so many member's homes this week. I've been able to see how Jesus Christ blesses everyone's lives-- and it's different for everyone. I know Jesus and Heavenly Father know and love you individually!
