The Gulfport Elders needed us to meet them at a park in their area that they could bike to Friday night so we could give them the car. Sister Robison was going home with the STLs for exchanges that night, so she had her bags and her bed roll with her as we waited for the Hermanas to pick us up. Sister Hollist really wasn't lying when she said someone told her the Sisters look homeless...

Thursday, 10/22-- We made a really cool friend last week and we had an awesome lesson with her on Thursday! Her name is Sharon, and she is deaf. We video called a Sister in our mission in Venice, (which is a place in Florida-- our mission boundaries do not extend to Italy) who knows ASL and interpreted for us. This was our first lesson with Sharon, and she is so amazing and so funny!

Friday, 10/23-- We had a great lesson with our friend Anthony on Friday, where he told us he wants to be on the right side of God. We told him that the best way to do that is through baptism, so we set a date for him to be baptized! He is so excited!

Saturday, 10/24-- A lot happened on Saturday! We started our exchange Friday night, and I stayed in St. Pete with Hermana Hull while Sister Robison got to skip out on bike day by going to the STLs' area with Hermana Nelson.

We had a couple really good lessons! We met with our soon-to-be returning member Amber, who is so amazing! She has been through a lot in her life, and she wants to give it over to the Lord to become a new person! Her desire to act on her faith is so strong, and she is so inspiring!

We also met with Debbie on Saturday! At the beginning of the lesson, we asked if she would be able to flip to some verses in her Book of Mormon so she could read with us. She stood up and said, "it's over here because I've been reading it," and went over to grab it. We went through the entire Plan of Salvation with her, which I'd never taught in its entirety in one lesson before. It took a bit longer, but the Spirit was so strong and she was so invested in it!

Sunday, 10/25-- So many people came to church; it was a party! Our friends Anthony, Debbie, Sharon and Phil came! Annnnd April came (it's always so fun to see her!), as well as Snow! Some sisters in the ward were talking to Snow after church, and they figured out that she actually lives down in Sarasota. She will probably start going to church closer to her, which is a little sad, but will be best for her in the end. She'll go make the missionaries and the ward down there really happy!

Monday, 10/26-- We did some service for our new bestie Jo-Lynne! She's the cutest ever! She is an older member of the ward that we initially visited on Sunday with another sister from the ward. We helped her out with organizing her room, and it was so fun!

Wednesday, 10/28-- So far for p-day, I've lost two pickleball games and made some sushi! We're planning on going to the St. Pete pier later today, so that will be a lot of fun!

Spiritual thought-- Helaman 5:12 (page 378 of the Book of Mormon)
And now, my sons, remember, remember that it is upon the rock of our Redeemer, who is Christ, the Son of God, that ye must build your foundation; that when the devil shall send forth his mighty winds, yea, his shafts in the whirlwind, yea, when all his hail and his mighty storm shall beat upon you, it shall have no power over you to drag you down to the gulf of misery and endless wo, because of the rock upon which ye are built, which is a sure foundation, a foundation whereon if men build they cannot fall.

Remember that Jesus Christ can be your Rock, and your Sure Foundation. He is there to support you when you need Him and when you focus your life on Him.

In my humble but accurate opinion,
Sister Christison
