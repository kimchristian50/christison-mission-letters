Thursday, 08/20
We had a really good lesson with our baptismal date Chris! He is so excited to have the Spirit with him always. Pray for him to be able to come to church this Sunday!

Friday, 08/21
We had Zone Conference!! It was really good; we talked about how we can find people's needs and desires that the Gospel of Jesus Christ can fulfill.

After ZoCo, we went to Wawa (a gas station) to get gas and have a phone call lesson with a friend. When we were pulling up, we realized our back right tire pressure was at 25, so it was a bit low. We started getting air and we realized it was actually really flat. So we called Elder Jenson (the mission vehicle coordinator) and he walked us through putting on the spare. 

I got to stand on the tire iron to loosen and tighten the nuts and I felt a little bit (ok a lot) like Mary Poppins. It was really fun.

Saturday, 08/22
We did service for our bestie April!! She's a returning member whose good friend and roommate wants to progress towards baptism! She's actually so amazing, I love her so much!!
