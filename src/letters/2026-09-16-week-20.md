True story! I wish I had gotten a picture.

Wednesday, 09/09-- WE HAD THE MISSION TOUR!!! It was so amazing! We had Sister and Elder Jeremiah J. Morgan and Sister and Elder Allen D. Haynie speak to us. I learned a lot about how to be the best missionary I can be.

Thursday, 09/10--- We were delivering cookies to some members, and we started getting closer to one family's home when we remembered that we had been there before. There's room for about two cars to pull in between the road and a gate (this part isn't relevant to the story; I just think it's a bad idea). We pulled up to the gate (we didn't have much choice-- the road was right behind us, as I just explained) not knowing what to do next. I started opening their records to see if there was a gate code on any of them (unlikely-- there wasn't last time we were there), when the gate opened. There wasn't a car behind us that had used a gate clicker and we didn't open it ourselves-- our Heavenly Father knew that this family really needed to be stopped by.

Saturday, 09/12-- We had a member meal with the cutest family ever! They had two little girls and I had to complement the older one's socks because they were ADORABLE! The younger one immediately ran to her room and came out with a bundle in her hands. She placed three pairs of socks on the table in front of me, proclaiming, "my socks are cute, too!"

Sunday, 09/13-- Aaliyah came to church!!! She texted us at 8:45pm the night before that she wanted to come but didn't have a ride. Luckily, we have the best member ever in our ward! She was able to pick up Aaliyah at the last second! Aaliyah absolutely loved church, too! What a miracle ✨️ ✨️

Monday, 09/14-- I got to go on exchanges with Hermana Koch! I was in their area (which overlaps my area, so nothing much changed), so I got to be an Hermana (Spanish speaking Sister missionary) for a day! I understood very little of what happened, but it was so much fun! We had a lesson with one of their recent converts and they fed us arepas. They were so good!

Tuesday, 09/15-- We put our friend Laquanda on date for baptism!! She's so excited to make her first covenant with God after reading the Book of Mormon and praying about it!

Wednesday, 09/16-- We had a fun district lunch at Cava and went to the St. Pete Pier!! T'was very fun. We took the tram back from the point and passed some dolphins! I tried to take a picture of them, and although I took many pictures, they are all woefully absent of aquatic life. I did, however, succeed in snaping a Florida Man in his natural habitat.
