Six. And we almost had to call the Elders for help. Stay tuned for the whole story...

I would like to formally apologize to my mother for necessarily making fun of her centipede problem. There was one at the top of the laundry shoot, and instead of vacuuming it up, she knocked it down into the pile of clothes in the laundry room. She left it there for a while, and it had disappeared by the time she got around to trying to kill it. Then she had to be constantly vigilant so she wouldn't get bitten by it. You're welcome for the requested apology, mom.

Last random thought before you get the rundown of the week :) On Wednesday we got a referral from a Beadley, which we thought was an interesting name. We texted, "Hi, is this Beadley?" His response after we told him who we were and if he'd like to meet was "Any time is fine. My name's Bradley."

Thursday, 07/30
We were soo busy that we had to have our dinner hour at 3:45. Needless to say, it was a nice hour to rest, but neither Sister Crowther nor I ate dinner during this time. I packed some food to eat between lessons.

Friday, 07/31
Story time!

We (Sister Frey, Sister George, Sister Crowther and I) had a surprise sleepover with the Hermanas Friday night! We basically went straight to their apartment after the stake volleyball activity (which Caleb came to!), so we didn't have time to grab anything. We showed up with only our proselyting outfits and bags. After we got there, we needed to take the bike rack off the car, so we all went down to do that. It was pretty stuck, so all six of us were outside trying to help. Fret not, we got it off! And we didn't even need to call the Elders :)

The Hermanas were awesome enough to let us change into their extra pajamas and sleep in their spare room on a blanket on the floor (there was only one air mattress and no one wanted to take it). None of us really slept-- we figured out in the morning that all of us were trying not to wake each other up throughout the night.

Saturday, 08/01
We had a great lesson with Bradley (not Beadley)! The member that joined us was so amazing! She shared her testimony so many times, and the Spirit was so strong!

We had another surprise sleepover! This time it was at Sister Frey and Sister George's apartment, so we had time to grab our bedrolls because they're in the same complex as us. They had air mattresses, so I actually got some sleep!

Sunday, 08/02
We had five friends show up to church! Arthur and Jeremiah came, as well as Treva's godson LaJay and our two new friends Caleb and Bradley (not Beadley)! Joel came to church too, and he shared his testimony in Sacrament meeting!

We got to sleep in our own room Sunday night!

Monday, 08/03
We did a member stop-by, which ended up being really fun! She lived in Albuquerque for a few years and graduated from Eldorado high school. It was so fun to meet someone who's actually lived in my hometown!

Tuesday, 08/04
Tuesday night, we dropped Sister Crowther off at a Racetrack halfway to Brooksville to fend for herself.

Ok, we weren't that mean-- the departing missionaries are going to the temple with President and his wife, and she got picked up by another last transfer Sister there.

Wednesday, 08/05
I'm in a temporary trio with Sister Frey and Sister George today! We went around downtown Ocala and we'll be double-covering tonight for prime time!
