We were stopping by someone, and their cat humiliated me in front of my companion by maliciously going through my legs. Cats really are evil.

On another, though similarly distressing, note, transfers are tomorrow! If you crack the code, you can figure out what's going to happen-- where will I be? what companion will I have? I put a lot of work into this, so I'd better get a response from you...

 Good luck!

Thursday, 09/17-- We had district council in the Seminole building (which is 30 minutes away from our usual building) because interviews with President for all the missionaries in the St. Pete zone was that day. For those who remember my emails enough to be confused-- I'm serving in the St. Pete ward, which is in the St. Pete zone.

Friday, 09/18-- The primary put on a talent show Friday evening! They were so cute, and so many less active members and friends of ward members that came! We didn't get a chance to talk with everyone, but so many people came away spiritually refreshed and uplifted!

Saturday, 09/19-- I had my first sick day(ish)! It was kind of horrible. My comp was feeling awful throughout the day and had to go home at 4pm. We had four hours of phone work to do anyways, so nothing much changed. I did want to throw my phone at the wall, though, because I hate looking at it for that long.

Sunday, 09/20-- We had the primary program! That's where the kids in the ward sing songs about Jesus and share their testimonies during Sacrament meeting. It is so cute and wholesome! The primary in the St. Pete ward is so small-- there were six kids up there, and one of them was from the Seminole ward.

Monday, 09/21-- Very interesting day. We've had this member referral (when one of our members recommends one of their frIends to the missionaries) named Jennifer since the Elders were in the area. We haven't been able to get in contact with her this whole transfer because we only had her address and she was never home when we stopped by. On Monday, she texted us out of the blue to ask us to stop by Tuesday morning!

Tuesday, 09/22-- We had our lesson with Jennifer! She had so many questions that we were able to talk about and answer with the Book of Mormon.

Wednesday, 09/23-- Today's the last p-day of the transfer! We had a cookout with the district, so that was a lot of fun!

Spiritual thought-- 3 Nephi 11:11
And behold, I am the light and the life of the world; and I have drunk out of that bitter cup which the Father hath given me, and have glorified the Father in taking upon me the sins of the world, in the which I have suffered the will of the Father in all things from the beginning.

Jesus Christ commands us to shine our light, and this scripture gives clarity on how to do that. When we tell others about the sacrifice and Atonement of Jesus Christ, we bring light into other people's lives.

May the sun shine on your conquests this day,
Sister Christison
